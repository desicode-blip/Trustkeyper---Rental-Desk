# Rental Desk – how everything works (tutorial)

For sales executives, admins and any AI agent picking up the work. Each section says **what you see**, **what you do**, and **what the desk does in response**.

---

## 1. Signing in
1. Open **rentaldesk.trustkeyper.com**.
2. **Log in** with email + password, or **Continue with Google**. New people use **Sign up** (name, email, password of 8+ characters).
3. After sign up, open the link Google emails you, then tap **I've confirmed my email**.
4. If your email has no access yet you see: *"Access has not been given yet… call +91 80885 16875"*. An admin must invite you first (section 11).
5. First time only: type your **name**, **phone** and pick a **language** (English / हिंदी / తెలుగు), then **Start**.
- **Forgot password?** on the log-in screen sends a reset link.

## 2. Moving around
- Left menu (top bar on tablets and phones): **Today's work, Matches, Tenants, Properties, Brokers, Follow-ups, Help & feedback, Profile**.
- The **Rental Desk logo** always takes you back to **Today's work**.
- Numbers beside menu items = calls waiting there.

## 3. Today's work (home)
One numbered list, in the order to call:
1. **Visit** (red tag): tenants with a visit booked; call before the visit.
2. **Share homes** (green): tenants with new matching homes.
3. **Follow-up** (amber): follow-ups due today or overdue.
4. **New lead**: leads never called.
Within follow-ups and new leads, the side that is short for matching (tenants or properties) comes first. Each row shows **🏠 Property** or **👤 Tenant**, then **Call** and **Open**. "Show all" reveals the full list.

## 4. Tenants and Properties lists
- Tabs: **Today's leads** (added in the last 48 h, not opened yet), **To call**, **All**. Leads already sent for matching sit at the bottom of To call under "Sent for matching".
- **Search** by name, number or area.
- **Sort by**: Relevance, Date added, Follow-up, Budget/Rent, Name, Last called. Tap again to flip up/down.
- **Filter**:
  - **Date added**: tap the first date, then the last date; days in between light up (hovering previews the range). Quick picks: Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month. **Apply** / **Clear**; the ✕ on the pill clears it.
  - **Sources**: Facebook, WhatsApp, Existing sheet, Other online, Manual.
  - **Contacts**: Golden leads, Has phone, Missing phone, Brokers, Sheet updates to review.
  - An active filter turns green.

## 5. A lead card
- **Header**: name, type, golden-lead star, added time, phone, quick summary, status pill and **Call**.
- **Mandatory fields** (marked ✱, red until filled): name, phone, budget/rent, societies/areas, still looking/available, home or room, rooms, furnishing, tenant type and move-in date (tenants) or flat number (properties).
  - **Budget is adjustable** (tenants): tick it, pick ± 2k/5k/10k/15k/20k or type a number. Homes up to the top of the range then count as matches, flagged "confirm the stretch".
- Other sections (Who will live, Only for you, brokerage, etc.) are optional.
- Everything saves by itself; the bottom-left shows **Saved online**.

### When the last mandatory field is filled
A soft chime plays, the card shows **Sending for matching…**, then *"✓ Sent for matching · N homes found"* (with **Undo** for 8 seconds; confetti the first time a lead gets a match). The status pill becomes **Matching · N homes** (tenants) or **Offered to N tenants** (properties). **Refresh matches** re-checks. If someone later clears a mandatory field the pill turns amber: **Paused · needs <field>**.

## 6. Logging a call
In **Log today's call**:
1. **What was discussed today**: type, or tap **Speak** to dictate (in your language), then **↵ Add** (or Ctrl/⌘ + Enter).
2. **Next follow-up**: date + why. When both steps are filled the call is saved to **History** by itself.
- Couldn't talk? **No answer** (follow-up moves to tomorrow), **Busy, call later** (asks when; sets the follow-up), **Not interested** (asks why, closes the lead).
- **About** (tenants with homes): pick the home the call was about; the note is also tagged on that home in Matches.
- **History** (right side) is read-only, newest first.

## 7. Calling with the AI listener
1. Tap **Call** on a card or in Today's work.
2. Pick the app every time: WhatsApp app, WhatsApp Web, Phone (linked mobile), Another calling app, or Just copy the number. Keep **Open the AI listener** ticked.
3. In the panel on the right tap **Start listening** (allow the microphone). First use downloads the AI once (~3 minutes).
   - Use the laptop speaker so both sides are heard, or tick **Also hear the other person** and share the WhatsApp tab with audio.
4. **What was said** fills in a few seconds behind the speech (English by default; tick to keep the spoken language).
5. **Picked up from the call** lists budget, rooms, furnishing, tenant type, move-in, pets, food, areas. Tap **Fill** (or **Fill all**) to put them on the card.
6. **Put this in today's call notes** copies it into the call log. Then set the follow-up.
Tell the person their call is being noted.

## 8. Matches
- Each tenant sent for matching, with **Homes for <name>** beside them (best fit first, reasons and things to confirm).
- On a home: **Share on WhatsApp**, **Pitch**, note, remove/restore, stage (shared, visit, visited, interested), **Deal done**.
- **Share on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with TK2's message and a tenant link (`/h/<id>`), and marks the home "Shared".
- Visit bookings and tenant replies appear on the home ("Visit booked · Tue 24 Nov, 1 pm"). **Mark visit done** asks for the tenant's 4-digit code.
- **Deals done** tab keeps closed deals; a renewal reminder is set for 10 months later.

## 9. The tenant's side (link sent on WhatsApp)
1. Tenant opens the link: photos, rent card, facts, "What happens next", broker.
2. **Book a visit · Free**: pick a day (next 7 days) and a time inside the owner's visit hours (at least 1 hour ahead), name, 10-digit phone.
3. Booked page: **4-digit visit code** to tell the broker after the visit, reminders, Google Calendar, Maps, **Cancel visit**, **I visited the place**.
4. After the visit: **Did you like it?** Yes / No. Everything lands back in the desk (section 8).
- **Not for me** on the page tells the desk early.

## 10. Profile
Change name, phone, language (the desk reloads in that language), change password (email accounts), sign out.

## 11. Team access (admins, inside Help & feedback)
1. Type an email → **Invite**. New people start with no leads.
2. **Edit access** for each person:
   - **Full access** (sees every lead) or limited:
   - **Show leads from our Google Sheet** on/off.
   - **Property areas** and **Tenant areas** (tap any number of areas; counts shown).
   - **Also properties / tenants with no area set**.
   - **Monthly price** (₹).
   - A yellow note warns if an area is also given to someone else (you can still save).
   - Live count: "they see N properties and M tenants".
3. **Save**. Their list updates when an admin desk is open (or within a minute once the server role is granted).
- Admin emails always see everything and can't be limited.

## 12. Google Sheet sync and photos (Help & feedback)
**Sync sheet now** reads the sales sheet with your own Google access: new rows are added, empty fields filled, changed details wait on the card for **Accept/Reject**. Drive photo links bring in up to 10 photos per home.

## 13. Help & feedback
Type or speak feedback; it's private unless you tick **Share with team**. Admins see all.

## 14. Screen sizes
Desktop (≥1280), small laptop (1024–1279), tablet (768–1023) and phone (<768) each have their own layout; pop-ups stay 16px from the screen edges. Preview several widths at `rentaldesk.trustkeyper.com/rtest.html?w=390,768`.
